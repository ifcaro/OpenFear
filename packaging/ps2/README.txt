OpenFear (PS2)

You need your own legally-owned copy of Atmosfear (an installed folder, a
raw CD-layout folder, or an .iso with "ATMOSFEAR" or "OPENFEAR" in its
filename) to play. OpenFear does not include the original game's assets.

OpenFear looks for your Atmosfear files first on a real CD/DVD in the
drive, and if there isn't one, on a USB stick (mass:), either at its root
or one folder down (e.g. mass:/ATMOSFEAR/). Keep in mind an unmodified PS2
won't read a non-original-PlayStation CD/DVD, so a burned disc needs a
modded drive.
