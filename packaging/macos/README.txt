OpenFear (macOS)

You need your own legally-owned copy of Atmosfear (CD-ROM, ISO, or an
already-installed copy) to play. OpenFear does not include the original
game's assets.

This download has 3 apps:
  - "OpenFear.app" - the actual game.
  - "Install OpenFear.app" - installs game assets. Must sit next to
    OpenFear.app to work.
  - "Uninstall OpenFear.app" - uninstalls game assets. Must sit next to
    OpenFear.app to work.

To install game assets: drag your Atmosfear folder/CD/.iso onto "Install
OpenFear.app". Double-click alone won't find anything on its own.

To uninstall game assets: double-click "Uninstall OpenFear.app".
